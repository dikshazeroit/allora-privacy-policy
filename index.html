Allora Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 July 2026

Welcome to Allora (“we”, “our”, or “us”). Allora is a curiosity-driven platform that enables users to explore topics, questions, and ideas in real time by matching people searching for similar content.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how your information is handled.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Name or username
  • Email address or sign-in credentials (Apple / Google)
  • Search queries, topics, or questions you enter
  • Messages or content shared during topic-based interactions

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • App usage data (features used, session duration)
  • Log data (IP address, timestamps, diagnostics)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Enable real-time topic matching and conversations
  • Provide and improve app functionality
  • Maintain platform safety and prevent misuse
  • Respond to support requests
  • Analyze usage to enhance user experience

3. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary for the purposes described:

  • Account information: retained while your account is active
  • Search queries: stored temporarily to enable matching and improve services
  • Messages: may be stored for a limited period to support functionality and safety monitoring
  • Usage logs: retained for analytics and system performance (typically up to 12 months)

You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information only in the following cases:

  • With trusted service providers (hosting, analytics, authentication)
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To enforce our terms and ensure platform safety

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent where applicable

7. Children’s Privacy

Allora is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services such as analytics tools, authentication providers (Apple/Google), and cloud hosting. These services have their own privacy policies.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the app and on this page with an updated date.
